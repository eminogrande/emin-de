This column tries to answer one big question, how consciousness works, and it looks at it through one specific lens. It doesn't claim to be the only truth, but it gives you useful tools to think with.

The names on my map are Rosenthal, Lau, LeDoux, Humphrey, Metzinger, Lycan, Brown, Thagard, T. Clark and Deacon. Each of them is an important reference point. And for every one of them you can ask the same three things. What does this idea explain well, what does it miss, and how can we test it better?

If you are not an expert, you don't need to agree with every claim. The best way to read this is to compare the models and ask which one explains experience, brain data and everyday life with the least confusion.
