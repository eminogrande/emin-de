## One question, one lens

This column of the map tries to answer one big question, how consciousness works, and it looks at it through one lens, evolution and the tree of life. It doesn't say it's the only truth. But it gives you good tools to think with.

## How to read them

If you are not an expert, you don't need to agree with every claim. I think the best way to read all of this is to put the models next to each other and ask which one explains experience, the data from the brain and normal everyday life with the least confusion. You do that for Dennett, and then you do the same thing for Godfrey-Smith, for LeDoux and for all the others, and you see where they agree and where they don't.

## What to take with you

If you are new to philosophy of mind, the one practical thing to take from this is that no single model explains everything yet. But every model adds a piece. And when you read across all of them, you get an understanding that is stronger and less dogmatic than what you get from one of them alone.
