# From Berlin to Zanzibar: The Journey Behind Zanzibar’s Why Not Nightclub

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6SN_Hae8FxY>

I grew up in Berlin during a time when the city’s underground scene was shaping global music and art culture. Between 2009 and 2014, I immersed myself in that world — from theater and independent events to late-night clubs that became cultural movements in themselves. Berlin was more than a city; it was an education in diversity, creativity, and collaboration. Its mix of art, politics, and multicultural energy influenced everything I’ve built since.

## The Shift: From Berlin’s Nightlife to a Global Perspective

When the pandemic hit and Berlin’s nightlife came to a standstill, I decided to travel. I spent the following years moving across continents, exploring how music and culture come alive in different places. Everywhere I went — from small local venues to major cities — I saw the same pattern: every community has its own underground heartbeat, its own rhythm of expression.

One trip led me to **Zanzibar**, where my friend and renowned DJ **Andrei Pushkarev** was performing at the *Soundscapes Festival*. On that dance floor, surrounded by the island’s ocean breeze and rhythm, I met another German — someone born in Ibiza — who shared a vision of opening a club on the island. We stayed in touch, and when he later decided to turn the idea into reality, I joined as a co-founder and partner.

That’s how **Why Not Limited** was born — a space that merges the creative intensity of Berlin’s underground and Ibiza with the warmth and authenticity of Zanzibar. It’s more than a nightclub; it’s a cultural bridge where locals and travelers meet through music, art, and shared experiences.

## Technology, Bitcoin, and the Path to Nuri

Alongside my passion for culture, my career has always been deeply rooted in technology. I started early — founding an *Unreal Tournament CTF* clan back in 1999 — and later studied IT and Politics, spending two years in Hong Kong.

Back in Berlin, I founded [*proud.de*](https://proud.de), an independent socio-political magazine that became part of Germany’s cultural record, publishing over 30 printed issues with a circulation of 1.5 million. The magazine even premiered a film, *Para Para*, at Berlinale’s Panorama section.

Over time, my focus shifted toward digital innovation and blockchain technology. I co-founded [*abend.net*](https://abend.net), an AI-driven ad-tech platform, and [*abend.com*](https://abend.com), a social event discovery app that helped organizers reach audiences globally.

Later, as COO and CPO at *aeternity Blockchain*, I joined after raising over $60 million in tokenized crowdfunding and contributed to the development of Liechtenstein’s Blockchain Act. I then joined *Börse Stuttgart Digital Exchange* as Chief Product Officer, leading the creation of Europe’s first regulated Bitcoin trading platform.

Now, my focus is on [**Nuri.com**](https://nuri.com) — a Bitcoin-first startup that brings the freedom of self-custody into everyday life. Nuri combines a Bitcoin wallet, virtual and physical Visa card, and Web3 Bank IBAN and SEPA integration. It allows users to spend Bitcoin instantly, add it to Apple Pay, withdraw cash from ATMs, and convert between Bitcoin and local currencies — including the Tanzanian shilling. The goal is simple: to make Bitcoin practical, secure, and usable everywhere.

## Life Between Berlin and Tanzania

After years of living fully on Bitcoin and traveling the world, I’ve found a balance between **Berlin and Tanzania**. Zanzibar, in particular, remains a source of inspiration — a reminder of how global culture and local authenticity can coexist.

Working from here keeps me connected to Europe’s tech scene while letting me stay grounded in a place where life still feels real — where you can touch the earth, hear the ocean, and see communities building things together.

The journey from Berlin’s clubs to Zanzibar’s shores — from underground culture to blockchain technology — has been anything but linear. But it’s all connected by one idea: curiosity.
