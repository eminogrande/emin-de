1. Mount Kilimanjaro
 - Height: 5,895 meters (19,341 feet)
 - Specialty:
 - Highest mountain in Africa and the highest free-standing mountain in the world.
 - Composed of three volcanic cones: Kibo, Mawenzi, and Shira.
 - Kibo is dormant; last major eruption occurred 360,000 years ago.
 - Unique climate zones, from tropical rainforests to arctic ice fields.

2. Mount Meru
 - Height: 4,566 meters (14,980 feet)
 - Specialty:
 - Active stratovolcano located in Arusha National Park.
 - Last eruption was in 1910.
 - Offers rich biodiversity, including forests, savannahs, and an abundance of wildlife.

3. Ol Doinyo Lengai
 - Height: 2,962 meters (9,718 feet)
 - Specialty:
 - Known as the "Mountain of God" in the Maasai language.
 - Active volcano that erupts natrocarbonatite lava, which is unique for its low temperature and fluidity.
 - Lava turns white upon cooling, creating a surreal landscape.

4. Ngorongoro Crater
 - Height: 2,286 meters (7,500 feet) (caldera rim)
 - Specialty:
 - Largest inactive, intact, and unfilled volcanic caldera in the world.
 - Formed about 2 to 3 million years ago from a large volcano explosion and collapse.
 - Famous for its abundant wildlife and a high density of game animals.

5. Mount Hanang
 - Height: 3,417 meters (11,210 feet)
 - Specialty:
 - Fourth highest mountain in Tanzania.
 - A dormant volcano located in the Hanang District.
 - Rich in endemic plant species and a popular trekking destination.

6. Mount Loolmalasin
 - Height: 3,648 meters (11,968 feet)
 - Specialty:
 - Third highest mountain in Tanzania.
 - Located near Ngorongoro Conservation Area.
 - Known for its stunning landscapes and rare plant species.

7. Udzungwa Mountains
 - Height: Up to 2,576 meters (8,451 feet)
 - Specialty:
 - Part of the Eastern Arc Mountains.
 - Known for high levels of biodiversity and endemism.
 - Contains the Udzungwa Mountains National Park, which houses several endemic primates and bird species.

8. Mount Rungwe
 - Height: 2,981 meters (9,780 feet)
 - Specialty:
 - Dormant volcanic mountain in the Southern Highlands.
 - Known for its lush rainforests and high-altitude grasslands.
 - Home to several endemic species of plants and animals.

9. Mafinga Hills
 - Height: Up to 2,320 meters (7,612 feet)
 - Specialty:
 - Highest point in the Southern Highlands.
 - Known for its rugged terrain and rich biodiversity.
 - Important for its unique flora and fauna, some of which are endemic.

10. Mount Guruguru
 - Height: Approximately 2,000 meters (6,562 feet)
 - Specialty:
 - Lesser-known mountain with significant geological history.
 - Part of the Rift Valley escarpment.
 - Offers unique rock formations and is of interest to geologists.
