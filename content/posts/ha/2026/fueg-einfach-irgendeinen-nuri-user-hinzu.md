Ina ganin na warware wani abu mai kyau sosai da safiyar yau. Yana da alaƙa da wani feature na Nuri. Ga shi ra'ayin.

## Feature ɗaya maimakon biyar

Ina son na saka Multisig, Guardians, Inheritance da Referral a cikin feature ɗaya. Ga yadda zai yi aiki. Zan ƙara username ɗinka a cikin Settings na app ɗina. Sai haka kawai.

Da zarar na yi haka, kana samun kashi 50 cikin 100 na Fees da nake biyan Nuri. Kana iya zama Guardian ɗina a lokaci guda. Wato, kana taimaka mini da Passkey ɗinka don in dawo da Account ɗina nan gaba. Kana iya shiga Wallet ɗin a matsayin Co-Signer. Ko kuma mu kamfani ne kuma muna son samun Multisig-Wallet tare.

Duk waɗannan features ne daban-daban. Amma duk suna kan wannan Frontend-Feature ɗaya. Ka ƙara wani Nuri-User kawai.

## An raba Fee

Fees da nake biyan Nuri, za mu fara sa su a wani kashi 2.1 cikin 100. Kowane lokacin da na yi biya, rabin yana zuwa Nuri kuma sauran rabin yana zuwa ga mutumin da na ƙara.

Kana iya ƙara usernames 10 ma. Sai ka samu Wallet tare da mutane 10, kuma ana raba Fees da 10. Duk sababbin mutanenka suna samun nasu Cut ɗin ta atomatik, kuma Nuri yana samun ɗaya cikin goma.

Tabbas, mutane na iya ƙara juna, sai kuma ya dawo wurina. Dole ne mu duba kada mu gina Loop a can. Tabbas hakan ba zai yi aiki ba.

## Mama ta samu Satoshi 17

Bitcoiners suna son su sa sauran mutane su zama Bitcoiners. Yanzu zan iya gayyatar mahaifiyata. Kuma kowane lokacin da na yi biya, tana samun Push-Notification. Ka sami Satoshi 20 yanzu. Ka sami Satoshi 17 yanzu.

Na san wannan daga kaina. A wani sabis ɗaya, kowane lokacin da Swap ya faru, ina samun saƙon Telegram, kuma a ciki yana cewa na samu Satoshi 10. Wannan shine abu mafi yaduwa da na taɓa fuskanta a raina. Yana da ban dariya, kuma muna ba da juna ƙaramin kuɗi.

Da Arkade za mu iya aika Satoshi 1, 2, 3, 4 ko 5 ba tare da Fee ba. Kuma wannan yana nuna yadda Arkade yake da ban mamaki a wannan batu.

## Expanding maimakon Decaying Multisig

Keys da muke ƙarawa suna aiki akasin abin da muke yi a Nuri. Idan ba ma so su zama Signers tun farko, to ba Decaying Multisig ba ne, Expanding Multisig ne.

Wato, idan na ɓace duk Keys ɗina, to bayan wani lokaci, shekara ɗaya ko kaza, Keys na Guardians ɗina suna kunna ta atomatik. A Bitcoin ana iya yin hakan. A Ethereum wataƙila zai yi wahala, amma tabbas yana yiwuwa.

Kuma tabbas, waɗannan Settings ne da za a iya saitawa. Babu wanda ya kamata ya samu kuɗinka kawai saboda ka ɗanɗana Bitcoin ɗinka na shekara ɗaya.

## Guardian da Erbe

Guardian feature ne. A can ina son in yi shi ɗaya-ɗaya kamar Bitkey. Sun gina shi ta yadda Guardians ɗinka za su iya taimaka maka ka dawo da Key ɗinka, amma ba su da damar shiga kuɗinka. Yadda suka yi shi daidai, dole ne mu duba shi.

Sauran feature shine Inheritance, kuma wannan yana da muhimmanci sosai. Ina son in shigar da 'yar'uwata a matsayin Erbin a wurina. Kowane lokacin da na yi biya, ana saita Counter zuwa sifili. Kuma idan wani abu ya same ni, duk da dalili, ban tashi gobe ba, to bayan shekara ɗaya za ta kunna ta atomatik kuma ta sami damar shiga Funds ɗina. Irin wannan abu yana da muhimmanci sosai kuma yana da ban mamaki.

## Fee yana cikin Frontend

Abu mafi muhimmanci shine inda Fee yake. A yau muna ɗaukar sa daga Providers, kuma wannan kuskure ne. Ya kamata mu sa Fee a Providers a ko'ina ya zama sifili kuma mu ɗauka a wurinmu, a Frontend ko a Backend ɗinmu. Bayan Nuri za su iya zama Providers miliyan goma, ba abu bane. Suna ɗaukar nasu Spread ɗin da nasu Fee ɗin, kuma ba mu ƙara komai a kansu.

Ta haka za mu iya bambanta tsakanin Nuri-Fee da Partner-Fee. Wannan kawai clean ne. Kuma ina son in nuna wa mutane a Open-Source-Code, duba, ga Fee, yana cikin Sourcecode. Ina son mu zama kamfani mafi gaskiya a duniya.

Labarin da nake fi so game da wannan shine taƙama a cikin Aave-Community, lokacin da aka canza Swap-Provider a bayan fage. Daga wannan ka ga cewa Backends ba su da Power kuma, Frontends ne. Wanda yake da Users, shi yake da Moat.

## Ka saita Fee ɗinka da kanka

Muna barin mutane su saita Fee da kansu ma. Kawai saboda adalci. Idan ba ka son biyan Fees, kana iya zuwa 0 cikin 100. Kana iya zuwa 21 cikin 100 ma idan ka so. Kuma idan wani ya tambaya, me ya sa kuke ɗaukar 2 cikin 100, sai mu ce, shiga Settings ka canza shi.

Me ya sa nake ganin hakan zai yi aiki? Saboda Partys ɗina a Berlin. Duk da Entry fee da na ɗauka kuma duk da farashin Beer, mutane ba su taɓa ganin yana da kyau ba. A ƙarshe mun ce, fuck it, Donation for Beer, Donation for Entry. Mun samu kuɗi kusan biyar ninki.

Idan ka ba mutane damar su saita da kansu, to akwai mutane da za su saita shi sosai sama. Saboda suna son Gamification ko saboda suna cikin Mission. Akwai Attajirai da Matalauta, akwai masu ƙazamin kuɗi da sauransu. Ni, ga mutane da na hadu a Conference kuma na ƙara su zuwa Account ɗina, zan saita Fee ɗina zuwa 21 cikin 100. Sai su sami Split ɗina ta atomatik.

## Kowane Call ƙaramin biya ne

Ina da ra'ayi mafi kyau game da yadda za mu ɗauki Fee. Za mu gate Nuri-API kawai da Micropayments, kuma Micropayments sune Fees ɗinmu. Kowane Operation a cikin App yana buƙatar ƙaramin x402 ko L402 biya. L402 idan yana da alaƙa da Bitcoin, x402 idan yana da alaƙa da Crypto.

Misali. Ajiye sabon Password a cikin Nuri-Password-Manager ko karanta shi yana buƙatar 0.1 Cent ko 1 Cent kawai. Kuma za mu iya ɗaukar sa kai tsaye daga Wallet, saboda yana agentic. A Router ina yin hakan tuni. Kana iya amfani da shi da Token ko kana biyan Calls, kuma za mu iya ba kowane Endpoint farashi daban.

To ba ka buƙatar gina Fee-System na ka. Kowa zai iya haɗa API ɗinmu a duk inda yake so, kuma muna samun Payments ta atomatik. Zan fara da Contact Book, 0.1 Cent kowane lokacin da aka ƙara Contact. Idan ya yi aiki, za mu faɗaɗa shi zuwa duk App ɗin. Feature ɗaya a lokaci.

## Family Wallet da Hardware Wallets

Idan mutane sun taɓa fuskantar yadda Safe-Wallet yake aiki, wanda muke da shi a matsayin kamfani, suna ganin yana da kyau sosai. Ina da Key ɗaya, amma biya za mu yi kawai tare. Wannan Crypto-Experience ne mai ban mamaki.

Ko Family Wallet. Mahaifiyata, 'yar'uwata da ni. Biyu daga cikinmu isa don biya tare. Multisig babu wanda zai iya tunanin sa. Amma a ce, mu mutane uku ne da Hardware-Wallet ɗaya, ko mu mutane biyar ne da AI ɗaya, kowa yana fahimta.

Kuma muna ci gaba da wannan Principle da ba wani zai iya yi. One-Click-Face-ID-Wallet. Seedless, babu Seed-Phrase, phishing-resistant, Multisig by default.

## Mataki na farko

Muna tafiya a hankali. Mataki na farko shine kawai, zan iya ƙara aboki a matsayin Padawan ɗina a cikin Settings kuma sai ya samu wani abu ta atomatik. Sai mu ga a Database ɗinmu wanda ya ƙara wa kuma waɗanne Swaps sun faru, kuma mu biya Referrals.

Kuma idan kana aika wani abu, ya kamata mu sami Autocomplete na duk Nuri-Users. Ba kawai Contacts ɗinka ba, duk kawai. Ina rubuta L kuma ina samun duk mutane masu L, kamar a PayPal.

Daga baya za su zo Key-Rotation, ƙara Keys da cire su da Hardware-Wallet. Dole ne mu yi duk waɗannan ko ta yaya. Amma daga Vision, ina ganin wannan yana da cikakke. Saboda yana da Feature mai yaduwa sosai, cewa kana iya samun kuɗi tare da wani ko ka zama Co-Signer ɗinsa.
